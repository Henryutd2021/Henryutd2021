# Honglin Li

Energy systems researcher · Postdoctoral Fellow, Electrical and Computer Engineering, Kansas State University

I build optimization and techno-economic models that show which low-carbon technology mixes deliver reliable, affordable energy, and who pays for them. My current work is on firm low-carbon power, including small modular reactors, for large new electricity loads such as AI data centers.

[Website](https://henryutd2021.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=O1BZdHQAAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-8405-3032) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/honglin-li-998b18221/) · [Field Notes](https://henryutd2021.github.io/field-notes.html)
